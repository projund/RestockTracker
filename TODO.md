## User settings and preferences

Design and implement signed-in user preferences after Firebase authentication is established.

Planned preferences include:
- Show or hide TCG, Squishies, and Beyblade
- Show or hide individual TCG games and Squishy brands
- Preferred retailers and stores
- Search radius
- Hide archived or stale sightings
- Default product sorting
- Hidden items management and restore controls
- Notification preferences

These preferences should eventually be stored per user in Firestore and applied across Dashboard, Products, Stores, Watchlist, Archived Sightings, and notifications.
